Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is CodeDev free to use?
Yes, completely free. No registration, no fees, no hidden charges. Browse all jobs and referrals for free.
How do I apply for a job?
Click "View Details" on any job card, then click "Apply Now" to go directly to the company's official careers portal.
How do referrals work?
Referrals are shared by employees at these companies. Click "Apply via Referral" to visit the official application page and mention the referral in your application.
How can I submit a job or referral?
Contact us via WhatsApp or LinkedIn with the job details. We'll review and add it to the portal.
How often are jobs updated?
We update job listings regularly. Expired jobs are automatically hidden from active listings based on their deadline date.

Want to post a job or referral?

Reach out to us and we'll get it listed quickly.
