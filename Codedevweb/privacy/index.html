Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

1. Overview

CodeDev ("we", "our", "us") operates the website codedev.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

CodeDev does not require registration or login. We do not collect, store or process any personally identifiable information (PII) such as:

  • Name, email address or phone number
  • Resume or CV data
  • Payment information
  • Location data

3. Job Applications

When you click "Apply Now" on any job listing, you are redirected to the company's official careers portal. CodeDev does not process, store or have access to any application data you submit on those external websites. Please review the privacy policy of each company's careers portal before applying.

4. Cookies

CodeDev does not use tracking cookies or analytics cookies. We may use browser localStorage only to preserve your search preferences within the current session.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external company career portals and social media platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

6. Google Fonts

We use Google Fonts to load the Inter typeface. Google may collect limited technical data (such as IP address) when fonts are loaded. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us via the Contact page.
